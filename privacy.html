Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Zenithal respects your privacy. Searches performed on Zenithal are processed directly in your browser and are not stored in any personal user account database.

Local Storage & Cookies

We use browser local storage solely to remember your light or dark theme preference. In the future, third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on visits to this and other websites.

Public APIs

When a query is not answered by Zenithal's internal knowledge base, Zenithal fetches public encyclopedia snippets over encrypted HTTPS connections.
