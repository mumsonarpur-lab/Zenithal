About Zenithal

Zenithal is a fast, clean, and student-friendly web search and knowledge discovery engine created by Abhinit Pandey in West Bengal, India.

Our mission is to give students and curious learners instant access to original science and math formulas, built-in calculators, voice search, and distraction-free answers.
